YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

This notice describes how information entered into this demonstration employment enquiry form is used. Before publishing, replace “the recruitment contact” with your legal business name and contact details, and have the notice reviewed for the laws that apply to your operation.

What we collect

Information you choose to provide, including name, date of birth, sex, nationality, current country, education, occupation, job preference, availability, criminal-record declaration, prior U.S. visa answer, visa type, passport number and expiry date, WhatsApp number, and optional email. These include sensitive identity details.

Purpose and recipient

We use these details to review your employment application. If you actively tick the consent box and submit the form, the submitted fields—including passport details—are sent to the configured recruitment recipient through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. Verify the organisation and recipient before submitting. WhatsApp/Meta processes messages under its own terms and privacy policies.

Documents and sensitive information

This form does not upload scans, National ID images, CVs, or other files. It does collect a passport number and expiry date, which are sensitive. Only submit if you understand and accept that these details will be sent to the configured WhatsApp recipient. Do not send document scans until you independently verify the organisation and its need for them.

Storage and retention

This starter project does not write submissions to a database or local file. The application data is sent to the configured WhatsApp recipient and may remain in WhatsApp message history or service logs. Limit access to the receiving account and delete messages when no longer needed. Vercel and Meta may process technical logs as described in their policies.

Your choice and rights

Submission is optional. You can decline consent and leave the form. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, contact the recruitment organisation using the contact details that the site operator must add here.
