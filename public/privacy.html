YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy notice

This notice describes how information entered into this demonstration employment enquiry form is used. Before publishing, replace “the recruitment contact” with your legal business name and contact details, and have the notice reviewed for the laws that apply to your operation.

What we collect

Information you choose to provide, including your name, email, phone number, nationality, general location, education, occupation, job preferences, availability, experience and optional message.

Purpose and recipient

We use these details to review your employment enquiry and contact you about potentially relevant opportunities. If you consent and submit the form, the information is sent to the recruitment contact’s configured WhatsApp Business number through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform. WhatsApp/Meta processes messages under its own terms and privacy policies.

No sensitive documents

This form does not ask for passport or national identity numbers, bank details, passwords, or document scans. Do not put these in the free-text fields. If identity documents are genuinely required later, use a separate secure process and explain why they are needed.

Storage and retention

This starter project does not write submissions to a database or local file. The application data is sent to the configured WhatsApp recipient and may remain in WhatsApp message history or service logs. Limit access to the receiving account and delete messages when no longer needed. Vercel and Meta may process technical logs as described in their policies.

Your choice and rights

Submission is optional. You can decline consent and leave the form. To ask about access, correction, deletion, or withdrawal of consent, contact the recruitment organisation using the contact details that the site operator must add here.
